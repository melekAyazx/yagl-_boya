Starry Night pretrained style-transfer model
Authors: Justin Johnson, Alexandre Alahi, Li Fei-Fei (ECCV 2016).
Project: https://github.com/jcjohnson/fast-neural-style
Model: https://cs.stanford.edu/people/jcjohns/fast-neural-style/models/eccv16/starry_night.t7
Reference implementation for OpenCV: https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/3.4/samples/dnn/fast_neural_style.py

Usage terms stated in the source project's README:
"Free for personal or research use; for commercial use please contact me."

The model runs locally. It transfers a fixed Starry Night-inspired style;
it is not a text-to-image or arbitrary-reference painting model.
